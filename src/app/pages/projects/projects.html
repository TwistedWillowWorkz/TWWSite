Reading Platform

Vellichor Nook

In Development

Vellichor Nook is a cozy digital reading companion designed for readers who want more than a simple book list. It brings together personal libraries, reading goals, shelves, reviews, stats, and the warm feeling of stepping into a quiet, well-loved bookshop.

Future plans include a mobile companion with barcode scanning for quickly adding books to a library, TBR, previously-read list, or wishlist. Community features are also part of the vision, including friend groups, shared shelves, and book club spaces where readers can choose a book of the month and follow the story together.

Living World Platform

Ashenwild

Conceptual Design

Ashenwild is envisioned as a community-driven fantasy platform for storytellers, game masters, players, and listeners. It centers around shared worlds, evolving campaigns, character journeys, and tools that help creators shape locations, encounters, lore, and living adventures.

The long-term vision includes companion tools for creating characters, tracking campaign details, and listening in on live public sessions almost like an immersive fantasy podcast. Listeners could gather, chat, react, and follow campaigns as they unfold inside a larger connected world.
