Building Vellichor Nook — Where the Idea Began

There's something magical about the feeling of wandering through a quiet library.

The smell of old paper. The soft glow of warm lighting. Shelves filled with adventures waiting to be discovered.

Vellichor Nook began with a simple thought:

“What if a digital reading tracker could feel less like an app… and more like a place?”

Not just another list of books.

Not another sterile dashboard.

A sanctuary for readers.

A place to organize stories, revisit favorites, track reading journeys, and eventually connect with other people who love books as much as we do.

The earliest ideas started as sketches, notes, color palettes, and late-night conversations about atmosphere. We didn't want modern corporate minimalism. We wanted warmth. Texture. Personality.

  • Old libraries.
  • Leather-bound books.
  • Candlelight.
  • Rain against windows.
  • Quiet corners meant for reading.

From there, Vellichor Nook slowly began taking shape.

And this is only the beginning.

Designing the Feeling of Vellichor Nook

One of the biggest goals for Vellichor Nook has never been just functionality.

It's emotion.

Most reading trackers are incredibly useful, but many feel clinical or transactional. We wanted something different — an experience that feels cozy, immersive, and personal.

The visual inspiration behind Vellichor Nook pulls from:

  • Old bookstores
  • Fantasy libraries
  • Parchment textures
  • Dark wood shelves
  • Warm gold accents
  • Deep forest greens
  • Rainy evenings with tea and a book nearby

Every design choice asks the same question:

“Would this feel inviting to spend hours in?”

Even the layouts are being built with comfort in mind:

  • Multiple viewing styles
  • Shelf-inspired organization
  • Immersive dashboards
  • Reading statistics that feel elegant instead of overwhelming

The goal is simple:

Make tracking books feel as enjoyable as reading them.

Why “Vellichor”?

The name “Vellichor” refers to the strange wistfulness of used bookstores.

The emotional chaos of finishing a book that changes you forever.

Certain stories become memories.

That feeling sits at the heart of Vellichor Nook.

Ashenwild — The First Sparks of a World

Every campaign begins with a single idea.

A map sketched on scrap paper.

A tavern filled with strangers.

A ruined kingdom waiting beyond the fog.

Ashenwild was born from the love of tabletop storytelling and the feeling that building immersive campaigns should feel inspiring instead of overwhelming.

Dungeon Masters spend countless hours:

  • Organizing notes
  • Tracking lore
  • Building encounters
  • Preparing maps
  • Managing player information
  • Trying to keep entire worlds alive in their heads

And despite all that work, the magic always comes from the same place:

The people gathered around the story.

Ashenwild is being imagined as a creative workshop for storytellers — a place designed to help bring campaigns together faster while still leaving room for imagination, improvisation, and personal creativity.

Not a replacement for storytelling.

A companion to it.

The vision is still early, but the heart of the project is already clear:

Helping people build worlds worth getting lost in.

Some of the best moments in tabletop gaming happen between the battles.

The quiet conversations at camp.
